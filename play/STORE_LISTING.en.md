## Full description (max 4000)
⛏ DRILL DOWN TO THE EARTH'S CORE – 6,371 KM OF REAL GEOLOGY

Take control of a tracked drilling machine and dig through soil, sediment, crust, mantle, outer core and inner core. Every layer has real depth, temperature, pressure and rock hardness – the deeper you go, the hotter, harder and more dangerous it gets.

WHY YOU WILL LIKE IT
• A drilling simulator with a real scale: from the surface to the centre of the Earth.
• Upgrade everything: drill bits, engines, armor, generator, emergency brake and a hydraulic erector with a tower crane.
• Depot tycoon: unload, store and sell your haul by freight train. Upgrade storage, conveyors, wagons and locomotive up to level 20.
• Learn while you play: a mineral library, volcano facts, a classroom mode and real historic events that appear as you reach their depth.
• Find secret relics and rare minerals, and fill your Hall of Fame.
• Atmospheric day and night, steam, sparks, lava and a calm original soundtrack.
• Plays offline. No accounts, no ads, and your save stays on your device.

HOW IT PLAYS
Drill, watch heat and wear, bring cargo home, sell, upgrade, and go deeper. Short sessions or long runs – the machine keeps its pace, and you decide when to dive.

Available in English and Hungarian.
